
KPI Dictionary & Data Quality Contract
Translate an ambiguous business request into measurable definitions and a testable agreement about what trustworthy data means.


Implementation steps
Choose a commerce, education, or operations dataset and identify its decision owner.
Define at least eight KPIs with formula, grain, filters, owner, and refresh cadence.
Profile completeness, uniqueness, validity, consistency, and freshness with executable checks.
Publish a data-quality contract that states failure thresholds and escalation actions.
Expected proof: KPI dictionary spreadsheet plus executable data-profile notebook and quality contract
